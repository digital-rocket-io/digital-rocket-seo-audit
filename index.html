Executive summary

The technical foundation is solid. The real gap is being found by AI, not Google.

This audit combines a full crawl of the live site with a separate manual review of how ready it is for search engines, answer boxes, and AI chatbots. The crawl came back clean: 92 out of 100, 1 broken links, 0 security issues. The manual review tells a more specific story. SEO sits at 55%, held back by a few concrete things like overlapping service pages and some missing schema markup. AEO is at 65%, mostly a handful of structured-data pieces that are missing. GEO is furthest behind at 44%, largely because there's no way yet to check whether AI tools even mention Digital Rocket. None of it needs a rebuild. The SEO, AEO and GEO tabs list exactly what's left to fix.
